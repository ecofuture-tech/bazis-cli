# bazis-cli 2.6.0

## Changes

- bazis-cli requires bazis-mcp 2.6.0 (`bazis-mcp>=2.6.0`): the tasks and the rules of
  bazis-front 0.3.0. A new product requires bazis-front 0.3.0; its screens are generated
  from their specs (`bazis_front screens`), with an extension (`extend`) or a custom screen
  (`custom`) only for what a spec cannot say, and its screenshots (`bazis_front shots`)
  are taken and looked at, at most three rounds. A change of a product generates its
  screens again and ends with the whole round of the final checks.
- The evals grade the screens of a product: `bazis_front screens --check` and `bazis_front
  shots --check` (a comparison with the specs, the screenshots not taken by the grader)
  with `design` and `e2e`, and no TSX of the product in `frontend/src/` but the code of its
  custom and extended screens (the copies, the generated screens and the template are not
  its own); the detail counts the lines of TSX written by hand.
